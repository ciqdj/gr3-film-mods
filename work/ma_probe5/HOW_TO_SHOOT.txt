MultiAxial probe round 5 - how to shoot (temporary firmware; flash films v15 back afterwards)
fwdc239b.bin SHA-256 0da550844c5dd7c222e282a4c1865a915a7f461caf6ed501dd82f1d0fd66ff33 (built on STOCK 2.10, no films)

Before flashing: note your C1/C2 settings. Afterwards the film entries are gone until you reflash v15.

1. Monitor: show hue_chart_<your monitor resolution>.png full screen (3840x2160 for the G81SF), cursor off the chart,
   Night Shift / True Tone / HDR / dynamic brightness off. Let it sit ~1 min.
2. Dim room, constant light, no lamp or window light on the screen.
3. Tripod, straight at the screen, chart filling most of the frame, all 4 white corner squares visible.
4. M mode, ISO 200, f/5.6, shutter so the white squares are bright but not blinking (~1/30). WB Daylight.
   DR / highlight / shadow correction OFF, clarity/shading 0, RAW+JPG, focus slightly OFF, 2 s timer.
5. Without moving anything, one shot in each Image Control:
   Standard, Vivid, Positive Film, Negative Film, Retro, HDR Tone, Cross Processing, Standard again  (8 shots)
6. Put the 8 RAW+JPG pairs in ~/dev/gr3-firmware/photo-ma5/
7. What to look for: if MultiAxial finally works, Positive Film shows coloured patches darkened in a pattern that
   changes with hue (bands of 30-85 % brightness), greys mostly unchanged. The other modes each switch one setting
   back to the GR III value; which of them still show the pattern tells which setting was the enable.
   If every mode looks like Standard again, the GR IV values are not the missing piece.
8. Quick check before the series: Positive Film live view on the chart - a visibly darker/patchy chart = it works.
   If a mode gives garbled colours, a frozen live view or an error: back to Standard (or pull the battery), note it, continue.
