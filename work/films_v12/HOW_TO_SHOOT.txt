Chart test (same setup as photo-v9):
1. Monitor: hue_chart_3840x2160.png (or your resolution) full screen, Night Shift / True Tone / HDR off, room dim.
2. Camera fixed, straight at the screen, all 4 white corner squares in frame.
3. M mode, ISO 200, f/5.6, ~1/30, WB Daylight, DR / shadow correction OFF, clarity/shading 0, RAW+JPG,
   focus slightly off, 2 s self-timer. Image Control parameters all at 0 (also for Bleach Bypass).
4. Without moving anything, one shot each: Standard, Vivid (Ektar), Retro (Gold), HDR Tone (Pro 400H),
   Cross Process (Portra), Bleach Bypass (Classic Neg), Standard again.
5. Put the 7 RAW+JPG pairs (+ FW_V12.BIN if made) in ~/dev/gr3-firmware/photo-v12/.
Plus a few real scenes in each film mode if you like (skin, sky, foliage).
